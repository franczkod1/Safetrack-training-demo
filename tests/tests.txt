# Tests

## Test 1 - v0.24 Documents page and employee hierarchy [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: full-page Documents workspace, upload control, employee hierarchy depth, fixed PNr alignment, v0.24 version
Description: Quickly verifies the primary v0.24 navigation and hierarchy changes.

Steps:
1. Open Mitarbeitende, expand Produktion and one Tätigkeit; verify the main group is darker, the Tätigkeit is lighter, employee rows are white, and PNr-#### values align in one column
2. Open Menü and choose Dokumente; verify Dokumente is shown inside the normal main content area, not as a modal, and Dokumente hochladen plus Erwartete Rückläufe are visible
3. Open Menü and verify SafeTrack v0.24 is visible

Expected: Documents is a normal app page and the employee hierarchy/PNr layout is clearly structured.

## Test 2 - Leader compares original and uploaded page before manual approval
Viewport: desktop
Covers: page-specific original snapshot, upload/OCR review, GET page review endpoint, side-by-side comparison, manual-confirm guardrail
Description: Verifies uncertain OCR pages have a real visual control step.

Steps:
1. Open Dokumente and click QA Vergleich vorbereiten; after QA Ergebnis shows Vergleich: ST-DOC-..., open that ST-DOC
2. Verify Prüfen & abschließen is disabled and open Vergleich öffnen on the page requiring review
3. Verify Digitales Original and Hochgeladener Rücklauf are both visible together with the comparison indicators, then click Seite nach Vergleich bestätigen

Expected: The leader can compare the original and scan before manually approving the page.

## Test 3 - Employee signed-document view filters by PNr on mobile
Viewport: mobile
Covers: multi-page completion, PNr page filtering, signed page URLs, employee profile document link, Alle Seiten anzeigen, mobile overflow prevention
Description: Verifies the employee profile initially opens only the page relevant to that employee.

Steps:
1. Open Dokumente and click QA Mitarbeiteransicht vorbereiten; note the employee name, PNr and ST-DOC shown in QA Ergebnis
2. Open Mitarbeitende, search the displayed PNr, open the employee, verify Dokumentierte Abschlüsse contains that ST-DOC, and click Dokument öffnen
3. Verify only the employee-relevant page is initially shown and Alle Seiten anzeigen is available; click it and verify all 3 pages appear without horizontal page overflow

Expected: Employee view defaults to the employee's signed page while the complete signed document remains available on demand.
