# Tests

## Test 1 - Complete three-page return passes strict validation and completes [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: POST /api/documents/prepare, POST /api/documents/:id/snapshot, POST /api/documents/:id/printed, GET /api/documents, POST /api/uploads/store, POST /api/uploads/:id/analyze, POST /api/uploads/:id/finalize, GET /api/documents/:id, GET /api/documents/:id/snapshot-url, POST /api/documents/:id/complete, GET /api/documents/:id/file-url, GET /api/employees/:pnr/completions, strict Seite x/y validation, QR/OCR per-page identity, employee completion link
Description: Uses the QA-only helper to create and return a genuine three-page SafeTrack document through the production storage and OCR pipeline, then verifies leader approval and employee assignment.

Steps:
1. Open "Dokumente" and choose "QA 3 Seiten vollständig"; wait until the created document appears with status "Prüfung durch Führungskraft erforderlich"
2. Open the document and verify the "Seitenprüfung" block says "Vollständig", Erwartet 3, Erkannt 3, no missing/duplicate/foreign/unreadable pages; open "Gespeichertes Dokument öffnen"
3. Choose "Prüfen & abschließen", confirm, verify green "Abgeschlossen – Mitarbeitenden zugeordnet", open "Unterschriebenes Dokument öffnen", then open the listed participant profile and verify the same ST-DOC appears under "Dokumentierte Abschlüsse" with "Dokument öffnen"

Expected: All three pages are identified by matching QR and printed ST-DOC, Seite 1/3 through 3/3 is complete and unique, completion is allowed only after leader approval, and the completed signed document is linked from the employee profile.

## Test 2 - Missing page blocks completion
Viewport: desktop
Covers: strict page-count comparison, missing Seite x/y detection, critical validation status, completion guardrail
Description: Verifies a document with pages 1/3 and 3/3 cannot be completed because page 2/3 is missing.

Steps:
1. Open "Dokumente" and choose "QA Seite fehlt"; wait for the generated document
2. Open the document and inspect "Seitenprüfung"
3. Verify it reports "Kritische Abweichung", shows 2 recognized uploads against 3 expected pages, explicitly reports "Seite 2/3 fehlt", and "Prüfen & abschließen" is disabled

Expected: The missing page is a blocking error and the document cannot be assigned to employees.

## Test 3 - Duplicate and foreign pages are blocking errors
Viewport: desktop
Covers: duplicate Seite x/y detection, foreign ST-DOC detection, QR/OCR conflict detection, completion guardrail
Description: Verifies duplicate pagination and a page belonging to another ST-DOC are both rejected as critical page errors.

Steps:
1. Open "Dokumente", choose "QA Seite doppelt", open the generated document, and verify "Seite 2/3" is reported as duplicated and completion is disabled
2. Return to "Dokumente", choose "QA fremde Seite", and open that generated document
3. Verify "Seitenprüfung" reports a foreign/wrong page or mismatching QR/printed Dokument-ID and "Prüfen & abschließen" remains disabled

Expected: Neither duplicate pages nor a page from another ST-DOC can be approved or assigned to employees.

## Test 4 - Thirty-page PDF limit is accepted
Viewport: desktop
Covers: client PDF parser, 30-page maximum, 20 MB upload policy, Documents upload UI
Description: Verifies the production PDF renderer accepts exactly 30 pages and the Documents UI advertises the new limits.

Steps:
1. Open "Dokumente" and verify the upload note states max. 20 MB per file and PDF max. 30 pages
2. Choose "QA 30-Seiten-PDF"
3. Wait for the helper result and verify it reports "QA PDF: 30/30 Seiten akzeptiert"

Expected: A valid 30-page PDF is accepted by the same PDF rendering function used by normal uploads, while the UI shows the 20 MB and 30-page limits.

## Test 5 - Mobile multilingual Documents UI remains responsive

Covers: GET /api/documents, GET /api/employees/:pnr/completions, mobile navigation, Arabic RTL, Documents dialog, PNr-#### format, horizontal overflow prevention, Seitenprüfung responsive layout
Description: Verifies v0.22 does not regress mobile, RTL, personnel identifiers, or document navigation.

Steps:
1. Switch the language selector to Arabic, navigate to "Mitarbeitende", expand one area and job group, and verify a PNr-#### value
2. Open and close one employee profile, then open "Dokumente"
3. Verify "Dokumente hochladen" is reachable, the 20 MB / 30-page note fits the viewport, and the page has no horizontal scrolling

Expected: Arabic RTL remains active, PNr-#### stays correct, Documents is usable on mobile, and no horizontal overflow is introduced.
