# Tests

## Test 1 - Mixed pages, later missing page, completion, and employee evidence [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: POST /api/documents/prepare, POST /api/documents/:id/snapshot, POST /api/documents/:id/printed, GET /api/documents, POST /api/uploads/store, POST /api/uploads/:id/analyze, POST /api/uploads/:id/finalize, GET /api/documents/:id, GET /api/documents/:id/pages, POST /api/documents/:id/complete, GET /api/employees/:pnr/completions, unordered multi-document routing, persistent expected pages, cross-upload assembly, employee document link
Description: Runs the core v0.23 lifecycle with interleaved pages from two documents, keeps a missing middle page open, accepts it in a later upload, completes the document, and verifies the signed evidence on the participant profile.

Steps:
1. Open "Dokumente", choose "QA Gemischt 1+3", wait for processing, and verify two ST-DOC cards appear: one is 1/1 complete while the three-page document is 2/3; open the three-page document and verify "Seite 2/3 fehlt – bitte hochladen" and disabled completion
2. Return to Dokumente, choose "QA Seite 2 nachreichen", reopen the same ST-DOC, verify 3/3 and final leader review; choose "Rücklaufseiten öffnen" and verify pages are presented in logical order 1/3, 2/3, 3/3; then choose "Prüfen & abschließen" and confirm green "Abgeschlossen – Mitarbeitenden zugeordnet"
3. Note the participant PNr., navigate to Mitarbeitende, search/open that employee, and verify "Dokumentierte Abschlüsse" lists the same ST-DOC with a "Dokument öffnen" action

Expected: Mixed physical order has no effect on routing, missing pages persist across upload sessions, the reconstructed return is logically ordered, completion is blocked until every page is verified, and the completed signed evidence is linked from the employee profile.

## Test 2 - Duplicate selection, manual page confirmation, and foreign identity are safe
Viewport: desktop
Covers: POST /api/pages/:token/select, POST /api/pages/:token/confirm, duplicate receipt retention, manual page approval, hard identity conflict, completion guardrail
Description: Uses one mixed QA bundle to create a duplicate page, a noncritical manual-review page, and a different document page with conflicting printed identity.

Steps:
1. Open "Dokumente", choose "QA Duplikat + manuell", wait for processing, open the two-page review document, and verify Seite 1/2 has multiple uploads while Seite 2/2 requires manual review
2. Choose one "Diese Seite verwenden" for Seite 1/2 and "Seite bestätigen" for Seite 2/2; verify the document becomes complete and final leader review is available
3. Return to Dokumente, open the other generated one-page document, verify a critical identity conflict is shown, and verify "Prüfen & abschließen" is disabled

Expected: Duplicate evidence is never silently discarded, noncritical uncertainty requires explicit leader confirmation, and conflicting identity can never make a document completable.

## Test 3 - Mobile Arabic Documents UI and 30-page parser remain robust
Viewport: mobile
Covers: GET /api/documents, GET /api/employees/:pnr/completions, client PDF parser, 30-page maximum, 20 MB policy, mobile navigation, Arabic RTL, PNr-####, expected-return UI, horizontal overflow prevention
Description: Verifies the mobile/RTL experience and the maximum supported PDF page count in one non-OCR regression workflow.

Steps:
1. Switch language to Arabic, navigate to Mitarbeitende, expand one area and job group, verify a PNr-#### value, and open/close one employee profile
2. Open "Dokumente" immediately from the menu and verify the v0.23 view appears directly with "Dokumente hochladen", "Erwartete Rückläufe", and the note "max. 20 MB", "PDF max. 30 Seiten", "Reihenfolge beliebig"; verify there is no horizontal scrolling
3. Choose "QA 30-Seiten-PDF" and verify "QA PDF: 30/30 Seiten akzeptiert"

Expected: The old local Documents view never flashes or replaces v0.23, Arabic RTL and PNr-#### remain correct, the mobile layout has no horizontal overflow, and exactly 30 PDF pages are accepted.
