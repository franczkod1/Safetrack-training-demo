# Tests

## Test 1 - Saved snapshot, completion, and employee evidence [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: POST /api/documents/prepare, POST /api/documents/:id/snapshot, POST /api/documents/:id/printed, GET /api/documents, GET /api/documents/:id, GET /api/documents/:id/snapshot-url, GET /api/documents/:id/pages, POST /api/uploads/store, POST /api/uploads/:id/analyze, POST /api/uploads/:id/finalize, POST /api/documents/:id/complete, GET /api/employees/:pnr/completions, employee completion synchronization, Dokumentierte Abschlüsse, Dokument öffnen
Description: Fast end-to-end proof that the saved print snapshot and returned signed page are centrally retrievable and the completed ST-DOC is linked from its participant profile.

Steps:
1. Open "Dokumente", choose "QA Einzelabschluss", wait for the generated 1/1 document, open it, verify Seite 1/1 is verified, then choose "Gespeichertes Dokument öffnen" and confirm the stored print snapshot opens
2. Choose "Prüfen & abschließen", confirm green "Abgeschlossen – Mitarbeitenden zugeordnet", choose "Rücklaufseiten öffnen", verify Seite 1/1 is displayed, and note the participant PNr.
3. Return to Mitarbeitende, search/open that PNr., and verify "Dokumentierte Abschlüsse" contains the same ST-DOC with a "Dokument öffnen" action

Expected: Both the original saved print snapshot and selected signed return are retrievable, completion is durable, and the employee profile contains the same ST-DOC under Dokumentierte Abschlüsse with Dokument öffnen.

## Test 2 - Mixed pages keep a missing middle page and accept it later
Viewport: desktop
Covers: unordered multi-document page routing, persistent expected-page records, cross-upload page assembly, missing-page completion guardrail
Description: Verifies one upload may contain pages from two different documents while the main three-page document remains incomplete until its middle page arrives in a later independent upload.

Steps:
1. Open "Dokumente", choose "QA Gemischt 1+3", wait for processing, and verify two ST-DOC cards are updated: one one-page document is complete while the three-page document is 2/3
2. Open the three-page document, verify "Seite 2/3 fehlt – bitte hochladen", and verify "Prüfen & abschließen" is disabled
3. Return to Dokumente, choose "QA Seite 2 nachreichen", reopen the same ST-DOC, and verify it becomes 3/3 with final leader review available

Expected: Interleaved upload order does not affect routing, the missing expected page remains attached to the same main document across uploads, and completion is blocked until Seite 2/3 arrives and is verified.

## Test 3 - Duplicate selection, manual confirmation, and foreign identity are safe
Viewport: desktop
Covers: POST /api/pages/:token/select, POST /api/pages/:token/confirm, duplicate receipt retention, manual page approval, hard identity conflict, completion guardrail
Description: Uses one mixed QA bundle to create a duplicate page, a noncritical manual-review page, and a different document page with conflicting printed identity.

Steps:
1. Open "Dokumente", choose "QA Duplikat + manuell", wait for processing, open the two-page review document, and verify Seite 1/2 has multiple uploads while Seite 2/2 requires manual review
2. Choose one "Diese Seite verwenden" for Seite 1/2 and "Seite bestätigen" for Seite 2/2; verify the document becomes complete and final leader review is available
3. Return to Dokumente, open the other generated one-page document, verify a critical identity conflict is shown, and verify "Prüfen & abschließen" is disabled

Expected: Duplicate evidence is retained until a leader chooses a copy, noncritical uncertainty requires explicit confirmation, and conflicting identity remains a blocking error.
