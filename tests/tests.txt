## Test 1 - Dokumente category hierarchy, search and status filter [sanity]
Viewport: mobile
Covers: automatic central document list, document-backed category → training → ST-DOC hierarchy, collapsed categories and training submenus, visually distinct hierarchy backgrounds, aggregated parent status chips with counts, blue Rückupload status, employee PNr/name search, ST-DOC search, training name/ID/date search, status filtering, urgency-first ordering, no-results state, responsive controls
Description: Fast verification of the changed Dokumente browsing model.

Steps:
1. Open Dokumente; verify Suchen and Status are visible, there is no Alle Unterweisungen dropdown, only categories backed by actual documents are shown, every category body is visually collapsed, and the right-side category chevron is a clean downward chevron
2. Expand the first category and verify its chevron rotates upward; verify category, training and ST-DOC layers have visibly distinct backgrounds; verify only trainings with documents are shown and every training starts collapsed with a clean downward chevron and no ST-DOC card visible; when mixed statuses exist, verify category and training headers show every contained status as separate counted chips in urgency order, with Rückupload ausstehend blue; expand the first training and verify its chevron rotates upward and its ST-DOC cards appear, then collapse it and verify those cards disappear completely; finally collapse the category and verify its body disappears
3. Open one document, note its ST-DOC ID and one participant PNr., return to the list and verify searches by PNr. and distinctive ST-DOC fragment find it; also verify a training-name/ID or date query works, then select the document's status and confirm the visible hierarchy recalculates without empty categories/trainings and parent status-chip counts reflect only the filtered documents
4. Enter zzzz-no-document-match and verify Keine Dokumente gefunden. appears; press Filter löschen and verify the hierarchy returns

Expected: Dokumente uses a compact two-level collapsed category → training hierarchy derived only from real documents; hierarchy layers are visually distinct, category/training headers show all present status buckets with correct counts in urgency order, Rückupload ausstehend is blue, ST-DOC cards appear only after opening a training, search and Status filtering recalculate summaries correctly, empty parents disappear automatically, and the layout remains usable without horizontal overflow.
